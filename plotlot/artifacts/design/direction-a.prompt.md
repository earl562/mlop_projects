## Canonical prompt

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop product UI reference, 1440×900
Primary request: Create one shippable-looking interface mockup for PlotLot Direction A, an Operational Intelligence Workbench optimized for the common acquisition and preconstruction decision. It must feel like a calm evidence desk where a builder-developer can verify a parcel, understand what can be built, see the governing constraint, test the purchase ceiling, and hand off the next blocker without changing modes.
Scene/backdrop: application chrome only; warm off-white technical-paper canvas with fine hairlines and subtle tonal panel steps; no photography and no decorative scenery.
Subject: a fixed 56px utility header; a narrow left deal queue; one dominant central evidence spine; and a narrower right decision rail. The central spine presents, in order, parcel identity, jurisdiction and coverage, zoning, setbacks and dimensional rules, overlays and constraints, capacity and maximum units, survey/plat/construction documents, comps, underwriting, and provenance. The right rail keeps the acquisition decision visible with maximum units, governing constraint, purchase ceiling, evidence completeness, one structured grounded opportunity brief, a blocker, and an agent handoff. A map must not be the focal point; at most show a small inline parcel-outline thumbnail inside parcel evidence.
Style/medium: realistic product UI, not concept art; implementation-clear; restrained editorial data density; crisp Swiss-like hierarchy; technical ledger and annotated case-file character; predominantly square corners with 4–8px functional radii; no card mosaic.
Composition/framing: full browser viewport, orthographic straight-on view, all primary regions visible. The evidence spine occupies the most width. Use aligned rules and shared baselines instead of floating cards. Preserve whitespace around the top decision sentence. Headings never wrap beyond two lines.
Typography: Geist-like clean grotesk for UI; Geist Mono-like face for folio/APN, source IDs, ordinance sections, hashes, and money. No Inter. Body text remains readable at realistic UI sizes.
Color palette: paper `#F4F1EA`, raised paper `#FBFAF6`, ink `#17211D`, muted ink `#647069`, rule `#D8D6CE`, deep moss `#285A46` for verified/available, ochre `#A56B1F` for conditional/blocking, muted brick `#9C463A` for conflict/error, desaturated blue-gray `#536B78` for links and focus. No purple, neon, or blue AI gradient.
Materials/textures: barely visible paper grain and precision hairlines; no glassmorphism, glossy chrome, glow, beach imagery, palms, aerial luxury imagery, or lifestyle photography.
